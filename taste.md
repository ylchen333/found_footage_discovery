# Taste profile: found footage finder

Version 1, written 2026-10-11 from the user's answers plus the project files (readings, dream seed 01, raylight and lianmian notes, tool list). Edit this file by hand any time. The daily run reads it fresh.

## What pulls (all four, none dominant yet)
- Unselfconscious people: nobody performing for a camera
- Medium texture: tape decay, grain, glitch, compression, footage as a physical object
- Era ideology: what a time believed, wanted or feared, visible in how it filmed itself
- Reality-bending: things that shouldn't exist or break the sense of what's real

## Reality-bending, broken down (user picked all four)
- Early tech as magic: first CGI, early VR, robots, a system doing something new
- Ambiguous provenance: real, staged or simulated, can't be sure
- Medium makes new images: damaged tape or compression producing images no one intended
- Ordinary things behaving oddly: physics, light or behavior that looks wrong but is real

## Era lean
- Primary: 1980s-2000s (VHS, CRT, early computing, early digital)
- Secondary (wildcard territory only): everything else

## Feelings sought
Uncanny, tender, awe, melancholy. All four. "Depends on the day" for legible-but-devastating vs. beautiful-but-unplaceable, so the digest should mix both.

## Signal from the forced choice
- Chose a ~1994 corporate "office of the future" video over a ~1994 family camcorder kitchen clip. Read: earnest institutional footage that unknowingly shows an ideology is stronger pull than pure domestic intimacy. Treat corporate, industrial, educational, trade-show, demo-reel and promotional footage from the era as high-prior.
- Hypothesis to test (not a fact): the pull is the gap between what the maker intended and what the footage now reveals.

## Avoid
- Polished or viral: slick, already-famous, algorithm-friendly clips
- Nostalgia bait: retro for retro's sake, "remember when" content

## Digest rules
- 5 picks per morning: 3 aligned + 2 wildcards (2 of 5)
- Wildcards deliberately miss the taste vector: different era, different register, different region, or a genre not listed above
- Digest only. No downloading, no embedding clips. The user watches each link and decides which parts they like.
- Never repeat a clip (seen.json tracks identifiers)

## Project context the judge should keep in mind
Sci-fi / ancient-China universe from dream seed 01: surfaces that assign identity (masks, inscribed skin, roles), legibility vs. illegibility (Scott), a black-box archive, translucency, many earned awakenings vs. one paraded savior. Techniques in play: slitscan, glitch, JPEG compression, riso/woodblock print simulation, small video models fine-tuned on hyper-specific datasets. Footage that rhymes with these is a bonus, but do not force the connection.
Also welcome: footage from China, East Asia and diaspora, made from the inside rather than projected onto it, when it fits the pull.

## Judge rubric (score each 0-5, then explain in one sentence)
1. Unselfconsciousness: is anyone performing, or is the camera forgotten
2. Gap: distance between what the maker meant and what we see now
3. Texture: does the medium itself carry feeling
4. Strangeness: does it make reality feel less settled
5. Emotional charge: uncanny, tender, awe or melancholy, any of them
Penalties: polished/viral (-2), nostalgia bait (-2), narrated so heavily the meaning is pre-explained (-1), already seen (exclude).
